# BADGER-X3D-Pro-ESP32
### BADGER DEEP LAB #001

[Demo Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-eZi4rttfmk&t=25s)
**⬆️ Click to Watch Demo - 1 Month of Work**
I'm not a software pro. I'm a tower crane operator, worked both domestic and international.
I don't want to do that job anymore. I'm focusing all my field experience on this.
I'm working on robotic firefighting systems. I didn't want a toy controller for the robot, so I chose the X3D Pro.
But there was zero working video on the internet. I had to solve it first.
After 1 month, I did. At 04:17 AM, servos moved.
⚡ **THE LIGHTNING MOMENT:**
While watching LEDFACIL's video, I saw he connected the USB port directly to ESP32-WROOM pins 16 & 17. That was it. No shield, just 2 pins. That night at 04:17 AM, my servos moved for the first time.
### Wiring
- D+ -> GPIO16
- D- -> GPIO17
- 5V -> 5V
- GND -> GND

> ⚠️ **Note:** This project took 1 MONTH of day-and-night reverse-engineering and is actively being developed.

### 🚀 What is this?
Logitech Extreme 3D Pro joystick decoded directly on ESP32-WROOM **WITHOUT any USB Host shield or extra chip**. Pure low-level reverse engineering.
